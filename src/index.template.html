Long-term progress and evidence

Foundation · first month

Build a simple morning protection and evening care routine, choose enjoyable activity and balanced meals. Progress means repeatable habits.

Months 2–3

Review comfort and adherence. If desired, take your own private photographs under the same light, distance, angle and neutral expression. Do not use filters. This app does not retain photographs.

Months 6–12

Review your routine with a qualified professional when needed. Visible changes vary, and normal aging continues.

Professional options · education only

Not recommended or selected by the app. Ask a qualified dermatologist about benefits, alternatives, risks and suitability.

Topical actives

Retinoids can improve some signs of photoaging but may irritate; vitamin C and niacinamide are supportive options. Do not start prescription medicines from this app.

Injectables

Neuromodulators and fillers address different cosmetic concerns. Fillers have rare serious risks including vascular injury, blindness and stroke. Never self-inject.

FDA safety guidance

Procedures and devices

Lasers, peels, radiofrequency and microneedling can cause burns, scarring or pigment changes. Discuss experience with your skin tone and healing risks; do not use this app to select settings or perform procedures.

Claims to question

Expensive creams, facial massage and supplements cannot be assumed to rebuild facial structure. Avoid miracle claims and before-and-after images with changed lighting or filters.
