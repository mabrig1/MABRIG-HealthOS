Privacy and safety boundaries
All examples are built into this file. No health data entry, account, tracking, persistent storage or background transmission is implemented. The optional public research gateway retrieves publication metadata only when explicitly requested. Reloading clears changes. Reports are saved only when you choose to export.
Live clinical deployment requires independent review, privacy and security testing, applicable regulatory assessment, validated medical evidence and professional oversight. Future SwarmLab connectivity must use a dedicated authenticated, consent-based interface rather than the engineering mission endpoint.
